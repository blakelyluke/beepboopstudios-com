How we build

Small studio. Big on care.

A few rules we actually follow, taped to the wall above the robot.

  • Tiny on purpose

    A small team means every quest, lesson and button gets a real look before it ships.

  • Fun first, useful always

    If it isn't fun, nobody sticks around. If it isn't useful, why bother? We aim for both, every time.

  • Privacy respecting

    We never sell your data, and our privacy policy says plainly what we collect and why.

  • Made for every stage

    First apartment or fiftieth birthday, there's always something new to level up. Everyone's welcome.
